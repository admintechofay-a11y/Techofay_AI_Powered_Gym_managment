# Image and Video Credits & License Audit

All photography and media assets used in **Techofay GymOS** and the tenant gym website templates adhere to commercial-friendly licenses (Unsplash License, Pexels License, and Wikimedia Commons Public Domain / CC0).

No watermarks, no copyrighted materials, and zero hotlinks are used in production. All images are locally self-hosted under `/apps/web/public/images/`, graded to dark high-contrast moody aesthetic, pre-processed into `.webp` and `.avif` at 3 responsive widths (640px, 1280px, 1920px), and guaranteed strictly under **200KB** per file.
