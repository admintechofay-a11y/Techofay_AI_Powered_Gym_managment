1. Project Objective
• Design and develop a powerful, secure and scalable gym-management platform for gyms, fitness centres,
studios, personal training centres and multi-branch fitness chains.
• Build the system as an API-first, modular and multi-tenant SaaS product so Techofay can onboard and manage
multiple independent gym businesses.
• Provide a strong alternative to established gym-management platforms by combining operational management
with CRM, marketing automation, advanced analytics and AI.
2. Core User Roles
• Super Admin / SaaS Administrator
• Gym Owner
• Branch Manager
• Receptionist
• Trainer / Personal Trainer
• Nutritionist
• Sales / Business Development Executive
• Accountant
• Gym Member
3. Super Admin & SaaS Administration
• Gym/organization onboarding, activation, suspension and tenant management
• Subscription plans, trials, billing and usage monitoring
• Platform-wide analytics, revenue dashboard and system configuration
• Feature flags, support/ticket management and audit logs
• Tenant-level settings with strict data isolation
4. Gym Owner Dashboard
• Real-time KPIs: members, active/expired memberships, attendance, revenue, dues, leads and renewals
• Revenue, membership growth, attendance, lead-funnel and retention charts
• Trainer performance, branch comparison and business alerts
5. Member Management
• Online/reception registration, unique Member ID and QR code
• Profile, photograph, emergency contact, documents and fitness information
• Membership, payment, attendance, workout, diet, trainer and progress history
• Member status: Active, Expired, Suspended and configurable custom statuses
6. Membership Management
• Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, annual, custom, trial, couple, family and corporate plans
• Personal training packages, discounts, coupons and promotional pricing
• Membership start/end dates, freeze, extension, transfer, upgrade/downgrade and renewal
• Automated expiry and renewal workflows
7. Attendance Management
• QR, Member ID, mobile number and manual check-in/check-out
• Optional biometric/RFID integrations
• Attendance history, peak-hour analytics and inactive-member identification
